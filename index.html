Company Profile

Founded in Canada in 2022.
Redefining Future Mobility.

The company specializes in developing flexible automotive safety systems and future mobility solutions. Softauto’s core philosophy is to utilize flexible front and rear protective structures to mitigate collision impacts, protect occupants, and enhance pedestrian safety.

Unlike approaches that rely solely on rigid steel structures, Softauto explores adaptive materials, pneumatic/hydraulic cushioning systems, adjustable inflation pressure, and controlled deformation technologies to create a safer and more sustainable mobility experience.

Theoretical Models

Three Thought Experiments.

Experiment 01

Rigid Vehicle Collision

Traditional vehicles utilize rigid steel frames. In a high-speed collision against an immovable object, the lack of structural yielding means the massive kinetic energy has nowhere to go. The result is a violent transfer of force directly into the passenger cabin.

  • • Massive collision force
  • • Dangerous impact transfer
  • • Structural failure

Experiment 02

Elastic Rebound Problem

If we make a vehicle entirely out of soft, highly elastic materials (like rubber), the impact energy is not destroyed—it is stored. The vehicle violently bounces backward, creating a lethal reverse acceleration that throws passengers into uncontrollable rebound motion.

  • • Reverse acceleration
  • • Rebound force
  • • Uncontrolled physics

Experiment 03

Softauto Flexible Safety

The Softauto solution balances deformation and dissipation. Our flexible structure yields to the impact, but intelligent internal air and liquid cushions absorb and convert the kinetic energy into heat and controlled leakage—eliminating the rebound and safeguarding the human inside.

  • • Controlled deformation
  • • Gradual energy absorption
  • • Intelligent safety

Patented Technology

The Core Softauto System.

The central innovation of the Softauto patent lies in its dedicated flexible protection systems. While the center cabin remains a rigid sanctuary for passengers, the front and rear extremities function as highly intelligent impact buffers.

01. Front & Rear Buffers

The patented system locates advanced flexible structures strictly at the front and rear of the vehicle. During a collision, these structures deform first. Internal air and liquid cushion systems absorb the kinetic force, releasing pressure gradually through controlled leakage to safely dissipate the energy. This dramatically reduces acceleration and rebound force, protecting both the passengers inside and pedestrians outside.

02. Adjustable Inflation

The Softauto system is highly adaptive. The internal air and liquid pressure values can be dynamically adjusted via the vehicle's onboard computer. Whether optimizing for high-speed highway driving, slow urban commuting, or specific comfort requirements, the system actively calibrates the stiffness of the protection modules to match real-time road conditions and safety requirements.

03. Environmental & Affordable
